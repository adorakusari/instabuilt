🏆 VILLAGE THRIVES!

Your wooden village is the talk of the valley. Beautiful houses, happy customers, and a forest that keeps on giving. That's sustainable building, InstaBuilt style!

HOW TO PLAY

🪓 Axe — click a tree to chop it for wood. Every tree felled leaves a stump that regrows.

🌱 Plant — click grass to plant a free sapling. Saplings grow into mature trees over ~8 days (rain speeds it up).

🔨 Hammer — click a house under construction to boost it +20% for 5 wood (once per house per day).

🏠 Build — pick a blueprint, choose a roof color, click grass to place. Builders work automatically; weather & night affect speed.

🧑 Customers — they want specific houses. Complete them before they leave to earn coins & reputation. Reach 1000 rep to win!

🌲 Environment — keep forest coverage above 70% for a green bonus. Below 30% and customers stop coming.

⛈️ Weather — rain grows trees faster, storms halt building and can damage work in progress.
