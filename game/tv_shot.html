🏆 SUSTAINABLE WOODEN CITY!

The world's first completely sustainable wooden city — built with wood that gives back to the forest. The CEO couldn't be prouder: "This is what we came here to build!"

HOW TO PLAY

🧭 Walk around — click a spot on the forest, or use WASD / arrow keys. You start at the campfire in your clearing, surrounded by trees.

🪓 Axe — click a tree: you walk over and chop it for wood.

🪚 Saw — click a tree: you saw the log into planks over ~2s. Houses need wood + planks.

🌳 Tree types — 🌳 Oak = strong wood (6–8) · 🌲 Pine = cheap wood, regrows fast (3–5) · 🍁 Maple = decorative, sells for coins (4–6) · 🌲 Ancient = extremely valuable (15–20) · 🌱 Young = plant it, it grows into an oak. Hover a tree to see its type!

🌱 Plant — click grass to plant a free young tree. It grows into an oak in ~8 days (rain speeds it up).

🔨 Hammer — click a house under construction to boost it +20% for 5 wood (once per house per day).

🏘️ Cherry Communities — chop your first tree and the 🏘️ travel button unlocks. It takes you to an open meadow with the same graphics — houses can only be built there. Travel back to the forest to chop more wood.

👷 Your crew — 🪚 the Carpenter walks the village and speeds up house builds, 🌳 the Forester plants trees across the forest, 🧑‍🌾 the Farmer grows crops that are sold for coins every morning. Watch them work — they talk to each other and to you!

🏠 Build — in Cherry Communities pick a blueprint, choose a roof color, click grass to place. Builders work automatically; weather & night affect speed.

🧑 Customers — they want specific houses. Complete them before they leave to earn coins & reputation.

🏆 The ultimate goal — build the world's first completely sustainable wooden city: 8 houses built, 1000 reputation, and the forest above 90%. Keep the forest healthy while the city grows — that's the InstaBuilt way!

🌲 Environment — keep forest coverage above 70% for a green bonus. Below 30% and customers stop coming.

⛈️ Weather — rain grows trees faster, storms halt building and can damage work in progress.
